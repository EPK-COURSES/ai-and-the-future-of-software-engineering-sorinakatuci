# Reflection

During my research, I was surprised to see how quickly companies are adopting AI tools to automate simple tasks. However, I realized that human skills such as communication and creativity are still irreplaceable.

I am concerned that new programmers may rely too much on AI and forget the fundamentals. At the same time, I see opportunities: AI can save time and allow us to focus on more meaningful and creative work.

Personally, I want to improve my teamwork and problem‑solving skills, because I believe these will keep me valuable as a software engineer even when AI becomes more powerful.
